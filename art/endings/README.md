# Ending illustrations

Four production stills generated with built-in image_gen for existing canonical endings. No audio or video was generated. Final WebP files retain the generated composition; conversion changes format only.

Shared direction: realistic painterly cinematic still, Soviet polar station, cold petrol gray and warm tungsten, restrained psychological dread, no captions or new canon.

- A.webp: three CRTs showing the same empty bunk, radio and one steaming mug; operator absent.
- B.webp: two operators facing each other over two plates in the station kitchen; faces outside frame.
- C.webp: analogue transmitter, spinning tape reels, witness papers, an operator's hand; public testimony.
- D.webp: living exhausted Sukhov in the kitchen, steaming mug, erased reels, shift ledger and open door to clear polar day.

The stills are mapped by sync_campaign_art.py and shown only after the corresponding ending is selected. They do not claim to replace the owner's future filmed media.
